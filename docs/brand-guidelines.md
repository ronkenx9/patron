# PATRON brand guide

## Core idea

PATRON is private crowdfunding for people who want to support the work without becoming part of a public supporter list.

- Primary promise: **Fund the work. Skip the supporter list.**
- Product truth: campaign totals are verifiable; PATRON does not publish backer identities.
- Privacy truth: public pledges expose amount and timing. Silent gifts remain private and do not count toward the bar.
- Voice: plainspoken, warm, precise, never breathless or cryptic.

## Identity

The PATRON mark is an open circle around a shared center. The opening represents a deliberate privacy boundary; the central point represents the work a community gathers around. The stem turns the geometry into a subtle `P` without relying on a literal monogram.

Use the mark with the uppercase PATRON wordmark. Keep clear space equal to the center dot on every side. Never add shadows, gradients, outlines, or crypto symbols.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#161613` | Primary text, dark fields |
| Paper | `#F3EFE5` | Main background |
| Signal coral | `#F05A3C` | Primary action and identity accent |
| Cobalt | `#3D5AFE` | Secondary editorial accent only |
| Moss | `#2F6E52` | Live/success states |
| Soft paper | `#E7E1D5` | Borders and quiet surfaces |

Coral should occupy less than 15% of a screen. Avoid purple gradients and generic web3 neon.

## Typography

Use Geist for display and body copy, with Geist Mono for chain data, addresses, blocks, and receipts. Headlines are tight and sentence-cased; technical detail belongs in small labels or expandable supporting copy.

## Imagery

Use tactile editorial collage: real places, shared tools, rooms, paper, light, and traces of participation. Keep people anonymous or outside the frame. Avoid coins, locks, shields, hooded figures, glossy 3D tokens, glassmorphism, and sci-fi dashboards.

## Interface principles

1. Lead with the human project, not the protocol.
2. Explain the two funding routes in one sentence each.
3. Put technical proof one layer deeper, never in the primary navigation.
4. Show live chain truth or a clear failure state—never a guessed number.
5. Keep one primary action per section.
